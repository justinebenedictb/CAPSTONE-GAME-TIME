============================================================

METHOD
------------------------------------------------------------
Random Search, 40 trials per model x version (trial 0 = Stage 5 setting), seed 11.
Top 3 trials re-trained with seeds [22, 33]; winner = lowest mean validation MAE over seeds [11, 22, 33].
Score: validation MAE (RPC, athlete-weighted). Training split for fitting, validation split for early stopping and selection. Test split not used.
XGBoost: up to 3000 trees, early stop 100, MAE objective.
GRU/LSTM: up to 150 epochs, early stop 10, MAE loss, output starts at training mean RPC.
Trials run: 276 model fits, 7.2 h of training in total.
